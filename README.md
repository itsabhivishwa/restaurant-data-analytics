# Restaurant Data Analytics & Insights Portfolio

A comprehensive data analytics project focused on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), pricing distribution strategies, customer rating behaviors, and geographic cluster analysis using real-world restaurant data.

## 🚀 Key Analysis Covered

### 01. Exploratory Data Analysis
* **Top Cuisines:** Identified the top 3 most common cuisines and calculated their overall market percentage.
* **City Analytics:** Tracked restaurant counts per city and determined which locations boast the highest average customer ratings.
* **Price Range Distribution:** Evaluated and visualized the spread of restaurant price categories through automated count plots.
* **Delivery Services:** Analyzed how the availability of online delivery options impacts aggregate customer ratings.

### 02. Advanced Operational Insights
* **Rating Spreads:** Visualized rating frequencies to determine the most common score ranges and calculated average customer votes.
* **Cuisine Combinations:** Tracked the most frequent cuisine pairings and evaluated if specific combinations yield higher customer ratings.
* **Geographic Mapping:** Utilized latitude and longitude coordinates to map restaurant distribution density and spot regional clusters.
* **Chain Benchmarking:** Tracked restaurant chains across the dataset and evaluated their popularity and operational performance.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
* **Language:** Python
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook (Anaconda)
* **Core Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
